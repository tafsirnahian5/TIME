# T!ME — user guide

T!ME keeps your time, your money and your habits together on your phone. There is no account and no server: everything stays on this device unless you make a backup and move it yourself.

## The six tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | What's running, today's three commitments, what's next on the calendar, paused work, money due this week, and today's habits. |
| **Plan** | Your tasks, as a List, a Board or a Calendar (Month, Week, Day or Agenda). |
| **Focus** | One work timer: stopwatch, countdown or Pomodoro. Plus plain timers that never count as work. |
| **Money** | Wallets, what you've spent and earned, bills you've planned, loans and savings goals. |
| **Ideas** | Loose notes and a bucket list, kept apart from the clock. |
| **Links** | Saved web pages, in your own folders. |

Your profile (**Me**), **Search** and **Settings** are at the top of Today.

## Adding things

Tap **+** on Today or Plan for the Add sheet: a task, booked time, a bill to pay, money in or out, a habit or a link. Typed text is kept as a draft if you leave a form, and comes back next time with a way to discard it.

- **Task**: something to do. Give it a due date and, if you like, an estimate, a priority, a project and tags. *Reserve a calendar block* sets aside time for it.
- **Book time**: time on the calendar, with or without a task. It must end after it starts. If it overlaps something, T!ME asks what to do. It never moves anything by itself.
- **Bill to pay**: a bill or expense you expect. Nothing is paid until you record a payment. Recording a payment later can be partial. A plan with payments can be cancelled but not deleted.
- **Money in or out**: money that actually moved: income, an expense or a transfer between wallets (with an optional fee).
- **Habit**: something you want to do regularly. It can be every day, on chosen weekdays, a number of times a week or month, or every few days. It can be measurable ("read at least 20 pages") or timed.

Anything can repeat. Each repeat is its own record, so paying October's rent never touches November's. For a monthly repeat on the 29th to 31st, you choose what happens in shorter months.

## Working on something

Start a task from Plan, Task Detail or Focus. There is only ever one work timer: starting something else pauses what was running and keeps a note of where you stopped. **Switch work** does this in one step and asks what to remember.

- Countdowns and Pomodoro phases end by themselves. When the time is up you choose: carry on, take a break or finish. A task is never marked done for you.
- Every stretch of work is a session. You can correct a session's times in Focus › *Session history* or on Task Detail. A change needs a reason, and the original times are kept.
- A task can have several blocks of reserved time (Task Detail › *Time reserved*). A block can be split, and you confirm where the second half goes. *Reschedule* replaces the blocks you haven't used yet and never touches time already worked.

## Money

- Balances come from each wallet's opening balance plus every ledger row. Transfers move money between wallets without counting as spending. Refunds count against the original expense. Loans are neither income nor spending.
- **Money › Overview** shows this month's picture. Figures that include plans say which day they run to, and overdue plans are listed separately.
- Budgets can be set for the month and for each category. Alerts come at 80%, when bills still to pay would take you over, and when you are over. Each alert comes once a month.
- Photos or PDFs of receipts can be attached to a recorded payment or expense. T!ME keeps its own copy, and backups include them.

## Habits

Tap a habit's day to mark it done. Hold it to add an amount, a note, or mark it as a rest day. Rest days never break a streak. Streaks, rates and charts are counts and shares only: there are no points, levels or scores anywhere in T!ME.

## Reports and Me

**Settings › Reports & goals** covers time, money and habits, compared with the same part of the previous period. **Me** shows five plain ratios (Focus, Delivery, Consistency, Planning and Money review), each with the formula it came from. With too little data to divide, it says *Not enough data* rather than showing 0%.

## Reminders

Reminders can be set on tasks, blocks, bills, loans and habits, with the time of your choice. Notifications offer snooze and done actions. Snoozing moves the reminder, never the deadline. During quiet hours reminders are silenced, never dropped. If the phone was off, missed reminders are gathered into one summary.

## Privacy and the app lock

- **Settings › Security & app lock**: a 6 to 12 digit PIN, with optional fingerprint. The app locks when you leave it, after 30 seconds by default. Turning the lock off or changing the PIN needs the current PIN. After five wrong tries, there is a pause that grows longer with each further batch.
- If you forget the PIN, you can reset it by confirming your phone's own screen lock. Your records stay.
- While the lock is on, widgets show counts only, with no task names and no amounts. Notifications hide their details on the lock screen by default.
- The app lock controls access through T!ME. It does not encrypt the database against someone with a rooted phone.

## Language

**Settings › Language & region › App language** switches between English and বাংলা. T!ME restarts to apply it. Only how T!ME reads changes: your own titles and notes, stored amounts, backups and exports stay exactly as they are.

## Backups

**Settings › Data & backup › Create backup** saves everything to a place you choose.

- Protect the backup with a password so it can only be restored by someone who knows it. **T!ME cannot recover a forgotten backup password**: without it, that backup is unreadable.
- **Restore** replaces everything on the phone with the backup's contents. A safety copy of your current data is taken first. A wrong password, or a backup from a newer version of T!ME, changes nothing.
- **Automatic backups**: choose a folder once, then daily or weekly. T!ME checks each new backup before deleting older automatic ones, keeps the newest 7, and never touches other files in that folder. If something goes wrong (the folder was removed, or the phone is full), older copies stay, and Data & backup says what happened and how to fix it. With automatic backups off, T!ME can remind you after 7, 14 or 30 days without a backup.
- CSV, bookmark (HTML and JSON) and calendar (.ics) exports and imports are for moving data to and from other apps. They are not backups.
